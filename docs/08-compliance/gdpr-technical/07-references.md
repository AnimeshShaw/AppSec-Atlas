# References and Resources

To further your understanding of the technical implementation of GDPR, consult the following authoritative resources.

## Official Texts and Guidelines
- [Official GDPR Regulation Text (EU 2016/679)](https://gdpr-info.eu/) - The complete, searchable text of the regulation.
- [European Data Protection Board (EDPB) Guidelines](https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/general-guidance/guidelines-recommendations-best-practices_en) - Official guidance on interpreting the GDPR, including technical measures, consent, and DPIAs.
- [ICO - Guide to the GDPR](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-data-protection/guide-to-the-general-data-protection-regulation-gdpr/) - Practical, technical, and organizational guidance from the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.

## Frameworks and Standards
- [ISO/IEC 27701:2019 (Privacy Information Management)](https://www.iso.org/standard/71670.html) - An extension to ISO 27001 tailored for privacy and GDPR compliance.
- [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework) - A tool for improving privacy risk management through enterprise risk management.
- [OWASP Top 10 Privacy Risks](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-privacy-risks/) - Web application privacy risks and how to mitigate them.

## Technical Tools and Libraries
- [Google Tink](https://github.com/google/tink) - Multi-language, cross-platform library that provides cryptographic APIs that are secure, easy to use correctly, and hard(er) to misuse.
- [HashiCorp Vault](https://www.vaultproject.io/) - Tool for managing secrets and protecting sensitive data, useful for pseudonymization keys and database credentials.
