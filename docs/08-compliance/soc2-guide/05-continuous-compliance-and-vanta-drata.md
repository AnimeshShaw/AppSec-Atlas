# 05. Continuous Compliance Automation & Vendor Management

## 1. Automated Compliance Platforms (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe)

Modern compliance automation platforms connect via API to AWS, GitHub, Okta, and Datadog to continuously monitor controls:

- **Automated Evidence**: Tracks MFA enforcement, disk encryption, and branch protection policies 24/7.
- **Vendor Risk Management**: Audits SOC 2 reports of third-party SaaS vendors automatically.
