# 06. References & Standards

Authoritative references, benchmarks, and frameworks for AI Red Teaming.

---

## 1. Standards & Security Guidelines

- **[NIST AI 100-2 (Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework)](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)** — NIST guidelines for AI security and robustness testing.
- **[MITRE ATLAS™ Framework](https://atlas.mitre.org/)** — Threat intelligence and attack taxonomy for AI systems.
- **[Microsoft AI Red Team Playbook](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/security/ai-red-team/)** — Microsoft guidance on planning and executing AI red teaming audits.

## 2. Automated AI Security Tools

- **[Microsoft PyRIT](https://github.com/Azure/PyRIT)** — Python Risk Identification Tool for AI.

- **[Inspect AI](https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_ai)** — UK AI Safety Institute evaluation framework for AI models.
